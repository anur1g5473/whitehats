## A Recent Cybersecurity Incident I Read About

**McKesson data breach (August–October 2026)**

McKesson, a major healthcare and pharmaceutical distributor, disclosed in an SEC filing that it found unauthorized access to third-party applications on August 25. The ShinyHunters group has claimed it stole around 284 million patient records, though that figure is the attackers' claim and not confirmed by the company.

**Why it stood out to me:** the access was reportedly through third-party applications, not McKesson's own core systems. It's a reminder that an organization's security is only as strong as its vendors and integrations, and that supply-chain and third-party risk deserve as much attention as perimeter defense.

Source: [DMARC Report weekly roundup](https://dmarcreport.com/blog/berlin-ransomware-extortion-mckesson-data-breach-boston-scientific-disrupted/)
